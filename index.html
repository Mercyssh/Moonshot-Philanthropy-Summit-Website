Why Attend

What Would It Take to Build Solutions for the Future?

India's most critical challenges – from climate change to financial inclusion to preventive healthcare – have not stalled for lack of funding or intent. They have stalled because incremental approaches produce incremental results. The Moonshot Philanthropy Summit is built around a single conviction: that a moonshot approach, one defined by scale, radical innovation and a willingness to experiment, is what will move these challenges beyond incremental progress and toward genuine, future-ready transformation.

The summit will also explore how behavioural science, amplified by AI, can help bold solutions be implemented at scale across India's complex and remarkably diverse population. And it intends to seed something the sector has never had: a new set of metrics and perspectives for CSR/Philanthropy decisions, and frameworks that allow moonshot projects to take off in the first place. This is the need of the hour, as the world and our country enter a new era in which uncertainty, AI, accelerated climate change and rising inequality together reshape our very ways of life.

About

The Summit & the People Behind It

About the Summit

The Moonshot Philanthropy Summit is convened by Ashoka University's Centre for Social and Behaviour Change to ask a bigger question of Indian philanthropy: what would it take to build the solutions our future needs? Over one curated evening in Mumbai, it brings funders, founders, scientists and policymakers into a single conversation about moonshot interventions, the science and technology that can scale them, and the investment frameworks that can fund them.

This is not a traditional conference. It is a working room: live case narratives, real-time brainstorming and a shared ambition to move India's philanthropic capital from incremental giving to catalytic investment.

Centre for Social and Behaviour Change

The Centre for Social and Behaviour Change at Ashoka University works at the frontier of behavioural science and its application to India's most pressing development challenges. Its teams combine rigorous research, evidence-based behavioural design and emerging AI tools to build interventions that change outcomes at population scale – across health, nutrition, financial inclusion and beyond.
